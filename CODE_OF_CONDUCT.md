# Code of conduct

This is a small personal project, and everyone who takes part in it is expected to be decent to
each other.

## What's expected

- Be respectful and patient, in issues, pull requests and reviews.
- Give feedback on the work, not on the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language about anyone's background, identity or ability.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam and off-topic promotion.

## If something goes wrong

Write to me at [abdullahrather19@gmail.com](mailto:abdullahrather19@gmail.com). I read every report
and keep the reporter's name private. I may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block
accounts that break these rules.

This applies to everything in this repository: issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions.
